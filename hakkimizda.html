Wilderidge Craft

A Passion for Leather,
A Respect for Craftsmanship

Wilderidge Craft is a reflection of our passion for handmade leather goods. Our brand was born from the vision of combining the finesse of traditional leather craftsmanship with the needs of modern life.

Every product starts with the finest raw materials and is individually shaped by the hands of experienced artisans. Using vegetable-tanned Italian leather, waxed threads, and brass hardware, we create products that age beautifully over time.

Our name "Wilderidge" symbolizes the wild beauty of nature, while "Craft" represents the creative power of the human hand. The meeting of these two concepts is the essence of our brand.

Our Values

Mission & Vision

🎯

Our Mission

To keep traditional leather craftsmanship alive by producing handmade leather goods — each unique and carrying a personal story — without compromising on quality. To offer our customers not just a product, but a value they will carry with them for years.

As an alternative to industrial mass production, we embrace a sustainable and ethical approach to manufacturing, giving individual attention to every product.

🔭

Our Vision

To become a globally recognized brand in the field of handmade leather goods. By reminding the world of the value of craftsmanship, we aim to make high-quality, long-lasting products a part of conscious consumption.

With every product, we aspire to bring the beauty of patient workmanship and tangible quality to the world, against the fast pace of modern life.

Production Process

How a Product is Born

From raw material to finished product, every step is carried out meticulously by hand.

01

Leather Selection

The finest vegetable-tanned Italian leathers are carefully selected.

02

Pattern & Cutting

Each piece is cut from the most suitable area of the leather using precise patterns.

03

Hand Stitching

Stitched using the traditional saddle stitch technique with waxed thread.

04

Final Touches

Edges are burnished, hardware is attached, and quality control is performed.
